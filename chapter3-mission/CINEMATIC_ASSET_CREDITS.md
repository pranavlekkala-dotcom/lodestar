## This cinematic adaptation

- Station: original game geometry and textures retained; added a modest docking adapter collar, port marker and small capture indicator at the existing side airlock. No replacement station, interior redesign or blanket mesh simplification.
- Relay: original geometry retained, uniformly normalized; solar-cell colors/roughness calibrated for the shared lighting, separate small service status lamps added. No panel separation or geometry reduction.
- Planet: original geometry, UVs and baked maps retained. Reduced broad fissure brightness, moderated color/bump/emission, added subtle fine rock bump, and made the thin cloud layer less opaque with stable transparency. No replacement planet or new large terrain pattern.
- Ascent shuttle: original supplied light-hull geometry and embedded maps retained, uniformly normalized; roughness refinement and a small forward capture collar added. Functional solar surfaces, dark windows and engine recesses retained.
- Background: unchanged source JPEG used in a single World representation; gamma/intensity/orientation adjusted for a restrained backdrop. Original CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license and HDRLabs provenance remain applicable.
- New staging, cameras, lighting, outcome animation, educational transmission graphics and editing were created for Project Lodestar with Blender assistance. No NASA endorsement, actual NASA footage or verified GMAT trajectory is implied. Films are silent for the game's audio mix.
