# Project Lodestar — asset credits

Tutorial revision 7 adds procedural crest deformation and slow cloud-dome rotation to the source world at runtime. The original HDR, reduced distant-wave mesh, Blender source and cinematic are preserved.

Tutorial revision 5: `distant-waves.glb` is a reduced runtime copy of the supplied water world's "colossal distant spectral waves" at Blender frame 780. The packed `overcast_soil_puresky_4k` environment is used as the visible sky. Browser water/wave and rocket-coating materials are approximations for continuity with the accepted intro; the source Blender file and cinematic are unchanged. Original asset credits below remain applicable.

Original project work includes architecture, guardrails and services, the Lodestar identity and painted facade artwork, camera choreography, original interface graphics, material adaptations, lighting, animation, finishing and original sound design/ambient score. The audio056 mix combines original synthesis with the CC0 field recordings credited below; audio055 and earlier used original synthesis throughout.

## Typography and sound

Typography uses the installed Bahnschrift and Consolas families. The 34-second48kHz score, low cinematic swells and electronic/mechanical textures were synthesized specifically for Lodestar. Audio056 also uses the following free CC0 recordings, excerpted, filtered, synchronized and mixed into the original score. No stock music or paid generation was used.

The revised shallow sea, distant wave range and contact ripples are procedural Blender Ocean/material work. Original screen-fixed HUD/telemetry, boot graphics and sound cues were authored for Lodestar. No paid assets or generation were used. Earlier terrain, night sky and holographic elements remain only in archived project versions.
