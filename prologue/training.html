THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE SCREEN

Check our working.

These are analytic teaching calculations using NASA reference values, not GMAT output. The fictional water world uses Mars as its reference dynamics model.

What is calculated?

Hohmann transfer: vis-viva velocities, two instantaneous burns, half-ellipse coast. Spherical two-body gravity; circular endpoints in one plane. No atmosphere, J2, finite burns or rendezvous phasing.

What is a design-case assumption?

The inspection goal (1.8× image scale within 170 m/s) is an authored teaching task, not a NASA operational rule.

Workshop allowance: 12 game credits, representing limited parts for this exercise. Small/large panels cost 1/2 credits each; one/two batteries cost 3/5; single/backup controllers cost 1/3; pointing and relay hardware cost 2 each. These prices are authored game rules, not NASA specifications.

2.10 kg mass limit; 65% initial battery, 20% reserve; 90% charge/discharge efficiency; six circular orbits in the long-shadow case (the Sun lies in the orbit plane); 80% solar pointing factor. Each battery is 43 Wh (BP4 reference ↗), with mass derived from the published specific energy.

Small/large array active areas: 0.02/0.04 m² per panel; masses 85/150 g. Fixed bus (structure, power conditioning and housekeeping): 750 g, 3 W. Attitude control: 180 g, 1.2 W. Radio: 250 g; receive at 2 W, then forward at 12 W. Status/Archive profiles each receive and then transmit for 60/600 seconds. Data rate is 8 kbit/s, a reference rate used by MarCO, not a verified link budget for this design. These are authored exercise inputs, not a qualified hardware combination.

Failure tests are deliberate scenarios: one independently connected panel or the primary controller stops after orbit 3. The backup case assumes independent fault detection and switchover; it is not a reliability prediction or flight-qualified design.

Contact uses body rotation, straight-line range and a 10° elevation mask. No validated radio link budget is claimed. The CubeSat is released into the carrier orbit; no CubeSat propulsion is modeled.
