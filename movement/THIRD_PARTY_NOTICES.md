# Source and third-party notes

Three.js is vendored for reproducible tests and the optional reference demo. Its MIT license is in vendor/LICENSE.txt and reference-demo/vendor/LICENSE.txt. The tested revision is recorded in package.json.

The procedural glove appearance references the user-supplied Satisfactory screenshots. No Satisfactory game model, texture, tool or sound is included. Code-generated meshes/textures and procedural contact audio are part of the prototype work directed by the user.

The optional detailed ship reference comes from the user's earlier FAR REACH project. It is a comparison fixture; it must not overwrite the separate ship agent's current game environment. NASA reference URLs in the former demo are background reading, not a claim that this is official astronaut training software.
