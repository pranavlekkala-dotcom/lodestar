# Project Lodestar: use of AI (disclosure)

Team: **Rockets Built Wright** (Mission Game Design): Aidan Ma, Thomas Heacock, Pranav Lekkala, Nathan Grondin, Collin Lee.
This file lists every known instance and use case of AI in the game and its promotional material. The same list is in the
page metadata (`<meta name="ai-disclosure">` and the JSON-LD block in index.html) and in the in-game credits.

## 1. Claude (Anthropic), used through Claude Code
- **Game code:** the engine page (lodestar/index.html): rendering setup, movement integration, interaction, hazards and
  checkpoints, Chapter 1 and 2 story flow, the hands-on repair mini-games (2D input with 3D models), the PDA, maps,
  screens, the intro film player, the branching endings and the credits.
- **Integration:** connecting the teammates' prologue films and Chapter 3 (CubeSat relay and rescue planning) modules to
  the ship; restyling their interfaces to the game's look.
- **3D content:** Python (bpy) build scripts that generate the ship, rooms, props and service hardware in Blender, and the
  procedural 3D models of the repair mini-games.
- **Writing:** drafts of dialogue, Mission Control lines, room descriptions and on-screen text, edited from the team's
  story; in-game science explanations.
- **Voice scripts:** the text of the voiced lines (spoken by Piper, below).
- **Testing:** automated playtests, bug fixing, checks of every route.
- **Media:** editing the trailer and the 30-second proposal video (cuts, captions, timing), code that synthesises their
  music and sound effects, review notes and remake prompts for the Chapter 3 cutscenes.
- **Documentation and publishing:** handoff documents, this file, packaging, and the GitHub Pages site.

## 2. Piper neural text-to-speech (open source, runs offline)
- Every in-game voice: Mission Control and the ship computer (`en_GB-alba-medium`), the Expedition One survivor and the
  PDA (`en_US-hfc_male-medium`), and the ending lines. A narrator voice (`en_US-ryan-high`) was tested for the proposal
  video and then removed in favour of the team's own voiceover.

## 3. ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Drafted the teammates' maintenance-repair design handoff (events, readings, safety rules, science notes) that the repair
  system was built from (see docs/maintenance_plan_v2.md). Its code was not used; the content was rebuilt in the game.

## 4. Teammate-supplied work
- The intro film, launch film, training-simulation modules and Chapter 3 modules and cutscenes were made by teammates.
  Any AI assistance used in making them is declared in their own handoff documents (e.g. the Chapter 3 handoff's
  AI-video prompt pack for replacement films). The cutscenes now in the game are Blender renders.

## Not AI
- Music in the videos is generated by ordinary synthesizer code (no AI model). Third-party 3D models, textures and sound
  effects are human-made assets under the licences listed in CREDITS.md and the prologue/Chapter 3 credit files.
